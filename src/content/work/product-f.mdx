## The real problem

Product teams switch between 50+ tools daily, and none of them share context. AI generates faster than humans can verify. Research dies in transcripts. Decisions vanish into threads. Every AI interaction starts from scratch because no persistent brain learns the team's decisions, data and strategy.

## The Brain

Beneath every f() module sits a secure, persistent memory layer organised into nine categories: company, product and team identity; strategy, research and data memory; a decision log, learned preferences and active context. Context is injected progressively across four disclosure levels, so each function gets exactly the memory it needs, and nothing it doesn't.

## Who it's for, and what it plugs into

Built for IC product managers, founders and CPOs, VPs and directors of product, and growth PMs. The evidence layer connects to the stack teams already run: GA4, Mixpanel, Stripe, Postgres, Google Sheets and CSV on the data side; Linear, Jira, Notion, GitHub, Google Drive and Slack on the workflow side.

## Why it matters

After a decade shipping products for hundreds of millions of users, the bottleneck is no longer building, it's coordinating judgment. Product f() is the bet that AI-native product work needs an operating system, not another point tool.
