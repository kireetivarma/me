## The problem

Product teams switch between 50+ tools daily, and none of them share context. AI generates faster than humans can verify. Research dies in transcripts. Decisions vanish into threads. No persistent brain learns team preferences.

## Why it matters

After a decade shipping products for hundreds of millions of users, the bottleneck is no longer building — it's coordinating judgment. Product f() is the bet that AI-native product work needs an operating system, not another point tool.
