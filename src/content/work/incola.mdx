## The problem

Australian renters face a well-documented problem: security deposits (bonds) often run to 4–6 weeks of rent, a lump sum that blocks access to housing for people who are cash-constrained. Incola's model, where tenants pay in instalments, removes the upfront barrier. The product needed UX that built trust fast, since users were handing over rental contracts and personal financial details.

## Approach

Started with user research on Australian rental anxieties and the regulatory context. Flow mapping established the minimum viable information architecture: what Incola needed to collect, in what order, without overwhelming an already-stressed user. Wireframes iterated through the trust moments: where to show fees, when to ask for documents, how to confirm the deposit was secured.
