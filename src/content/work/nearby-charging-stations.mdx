## The problem

OnePlus users walking through an airport had no way of knowing a OnePlus Charging Station was nearby, maps apps aren't built to surface a single gate-side amenity. A Marketing × Software OS collaboration scoped a focused pilot: turn the phone itself into the discovery layer at the gate.
