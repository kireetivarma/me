## The real problem

Petfolk is India's new player in pet grooming services. Before this engagement the team had spent heavily building native apps on Android and iOS, and failed to maintain them without a tech team. The fallback was running a pet booking service on Shopify pages, chosen with a future commerce play in mind, but the problems were plain: visitors to the website had nothing to do, there was no booking schedule, and users were routed to a WhatsApp account.

## What was built

A pet-grooming booking platform custom-built for Petfolk. Customers can view all branches across India (Stores, Pods and Vans), browse the catalog, book a service and get a response from operations. Logged-in customers add their pet's info and see a catalog tailored to their pets, and can opt for live streaming of grooming sessions. Franchisee owners, staff and groomers are onboarded onto the same platform for seamless operations, with ecommerce next in the pipeline. Built entirely with Claude on Emergent.
