## The problem

Food and quick-commerce ordering required multiple app-switching steps, and the most valuable, highest-frequency partner apps had no home on the ambient layer of the phone. Shelf had evolved to support commerce widgets but needed daily-frequency partners to prove the model, and the live-activities surface (a glanceable, Dynamic-Island-style space) needed a partner standard before it could open up beyond OnePlus's own apps.

## Two phases

**Phase 1, Shelf widgets (OxygenOS 15).** Zomato and Blinkit launched as commerce widgets on Shelf: order tracking, reordering and quick-commerce access from the homescreen layer, run as two simultaneous partner integration tracks on the Shelf commerce SDK and launched as the "Eternal Never Settles" campaign.

**Phase 2, Live Alerts (OxygenOS 16).** The live-activities space, OnePlus's glanceable Dynamic-Island-style surface, opened beyond first-party apps to more partner categories: food delivery (Zomato, Blinkit, Swiggy), transit (Uber, Rapido, Ola) and music. This was orchestrated from my work with the India business-development team, global BD, R&D teams, marketing, and the partners' product, business and development teams, aligning global-partnerships value with the live-activities surface.
