## The real problem

Gig workers don't lack income, they lack the tooling and financial literacy to file returns, claim deductions and stay compliant. Talking to 100 drivers surfaced the real constraints: low digital literacy, forgotten passwords, fear of official-looking forms, and a daily life spent inside one or two cab apps. Above the drivers sat an agent workflow run on vulnerable Excel sheets.

## What was built

A minimal, guided filing flow for drivers, plus an agent dashboard that replaced the Excel-sheet operation behind them. The roadmap extended to vernacular languages, a payment gateway and team chat.
