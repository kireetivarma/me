## The real problem

Pre-production runs on fragmented tools and personal networks: casting calls circulate as posters and WhatsApp forwards, crew hiring lives in spreadsheets, and auditions mean travel or unstructured video files. Talent without connections never hears about the work; production houses drown in unstructured applications.
