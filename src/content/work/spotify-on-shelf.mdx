## The real problem

Music listeners interrupted whatever they were doing to unlock the phone and manage playback. Shelf had the real estate but no high-quality media integration that justified opening it. For Spotify, the prize was different: low-friction onboarding for users who didn't have the app yet. The product had to earn both outcomes at once, which is what makes partnership PM work its own discipline: you are managing two roadmaps, two success metrics, and one surface.

## What was built

Playback controls, album art and now-playing UI on a card users could place at 2x2 or 4x2; playlist pivoting and track-liking without opening the app. Under it, a media-widget SDK that made Shelf's audio slot reusable for future partners. The rollout went through beta with the OnePlus community before staged global release on OxygenOS 12, announced on the OnePlus Forum.
