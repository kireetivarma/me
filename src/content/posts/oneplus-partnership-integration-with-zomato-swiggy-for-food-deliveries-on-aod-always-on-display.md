In a world where convenience is paramount, OnePlus has taken a significant step forward by partnering with top food delivery platforms Zomato and Swiggy in India. Launched in February 2023 as part of the OxygenOS 13 update, this integration enables users to view their food delivery status on the Always-On Display (AOD) of their devices. This innovation not only enhances user experience but also simplifies the way we interact with food deliveries, making it an exciting development for OnePlus users across India.

## The Need for Immediate Information

In the fast-paced lifestyle that many lead today, quick access to information can make a world of difference. Traditionally, checking the status of a food delivery meant unlocking your phone and navigating through an app. However, with OnePlus’ new feature, users can now glance at their AOD and get real-time updates without having to dig through their phones.

For instance, with a simple glance, users can see if their food is on its way, delayed, or has been delivered. This feature is particularly beneficial during busy periods when waiting for food can feel excruciating. Instead of ceaselessly checking your phone, a quick look at your AOD saves time and keeps you informed.

*A visual representation of OnePlus smartphone showcasing the AOD feature for food delivery status.*

## How It Works

Enabling food delivery updates on the AOD is simple, just enable food delivery services in AOD settings. After ordering from Zomato or Swiggy through their respective apps, OnePlus users will automatically receive notifications on their AOD. The integration is seamless, designed to work effortlessly without requiring additional settings from the user after the initial setup.

The AOD feature will display relevant information such as estimated delivery time, the current status of your order, and a brief notification when the food has been delivered. This real-time update keeps users informed, providing peace of mind.

## Benefits of this Partnership for OnePlus & partners

The collaboration between OnePlus, Zomato, and Swiggy brings several notable benefits:

### Enhanced User Experience

By providing delivery details at a glance, the integration transforms the user experience positively. It minimizes the hassle of app navigation, which can be particularly daunting amid multitasking.

### Increased Efficiency

This feature significantly boosts efficiency. For busy professionals or students, it's one less thing to manage when scheduling their day. AOD allows them to stay focused on their tasks while keeping an eye on their food delivery status.

### Higher Engagement

The partnership also holds the potential for increased engagement for both OnePlus and the food delivery apps. Users who order food frequently will find this feature not only useful but also integral to their daily routines, potentially leading to more frequent orders on these services.
